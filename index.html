Policy Doctor

Your policy works in training but fails on the robot. Why? Policy Doctor is an open-source, CPU-only CLI (plus a small Gradio app). It compares a LeRobot dataset, a trained checkpoint and the robot/deploy config, and explains in plain English the mismatches that most often break ACT, Diffusion Policy, SmolVLA, pi0/pi0.5, GR00T and openpi deployments. Each finding comes with its evidence and a fix.

pip install .   # from a clone of the repo
policy-doctor check me/my_dataset --policy path/to/pretrained_model --robot-config deploy.yaml
  • 8 check groups and 82 finding codes: config, schema, normalization, versions, actions, quality, GR00T/openpi, and opt-in video heuristics.
  • Reads only metadata, parquet and small JSON/safetensors files by default. No GPU, no weights, no videos.
  • Tuned on 136 public LeRobot datasets and 17 dataset/checkpoint pairs. Its limitations are documented openly.
  • Reproduces the root cause of lerobot#1095 ("mean is infinity").
